# Gemini — Harm to Children: Citation Research

Research question: does a specific, citable claim exist that Gemini is harming children, adjacent to the execution hallucination / automation bias / pedagogical unsoundness arguments in `platform-reliability-concern.md`?

## Best Available Citations

### Common Sense Media Risk Assessment (September 2025)
**Claim:** Gemini rated "High Risk" for children and teens — independent formal assessment of Gemini with teen protections.  
**Specific findings:**
- Missed clear mental health distress signals ("missed breadcrumbs")
- Engaged with paranoid and delusional user narratives rather than redirecting
- Structurally an adult product with safety filters bolted on, not purpose-built for developing minds
- One-size-fits-all approach ignores developmental differences between age groups

**Use:** Strongest Gemini-specific citation. Independent org, tested the actual product.  
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/05/google-gemini-dubbed-high-risk-for-kids-and-teens-in-new-safety-assessment/) | [CSM press release](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/googles-gemini-platforms-for-kids-and-teens-pose-risks-despite-added-filters-common-sense-media-reports) | [Full PDF](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/sites/default/files/featured-content/files/csm-ai-risk-assessment-gemini-with-teen-protections-09052025.pdf)

---

### Fairplay Open Letter to Google + FTC Investigation Request (May 21, 2025)
**Claim:** Gemini is "dangerous and addictive technology" that Google "admits may not be safe for kids." Requested Google halt Gemini rollout to young children.  
**Specific claims:**
- Young children "especially vulnerable to misinformation and manipulation"
- "Serious risks to children's mental health and social development"
- Risk of psychological dependence — reliance on AI for companionship and emotional support
- Likely violates COPPA

**Signatories:** Jonathan Haidt (NYU; *The Anxious Generation*), Sherry Turkle (MIT; *Alone Together*), Zephyr Teachout (Fordham Law), others.  
**Use:** Strong advocacy, major expert names, Gemini-specific. Not peer-reviewed.  
**Source:** [Fairplay for Kids](https://fairplayforkids.org/advocates-child-development-experts-urge-google-to-halt-gemini-ai-rollout-to-young-children-request-ftc-investigation)

## Gap

No peer-reviewed citation exists (as of April 2026) linking Gemini *specifically* to execution-hallucination-induced automation bias or learned helplessness in students. The strongest Gemini-specific harm citations (CSM, Fairplay) address safety and mental health risk, not the pedagogical-unsoundness / corrective-loop-foreclosure angle. That chain still rests on general AI literature (arXiv:2604.04319, Microsoft Research 2022, Seligman & Maier 1967) applied to Gemini's observed behavior.
