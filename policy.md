# School AI Policy

## Philosophy

The School educates students in mind, body, and spirit to develop people of good character. Our academic program, which instills essential skills, knowledge, and core institutional values, complements this mission. The School recognizes the transformative potential of artificial intelligence as a tool to enhance student learning and productivity. As the School embraces emerging technologies, we are committed to guiding students toward a thoughtful, mission-aligned use of AI that supports their educational development.

While the School knows there are discrete skills connected to the productive use of artificial intelligence, we believe that in high school and middle school, AI should primarily serve as a learning assistant that supports individualized instruction and promotes deeper exploration of academic material. Where appropriate, we will build opportunities within the curriculum for our students to learn to use AI ethically and in ways that foster their cognitive development.

In our view, the effective and ethical use of AI requires creative and critical thinkers, discerning readers and writers, and individuals who can work as a team and negotiate conflict. It requires well-educated young people of good character.

## Community Ethical Use Statement

Members of the School community are expected to use AI and other technologies ethically and responsibly. All AI use must conform to the expectations of the School's faculty and staff code of conduct and student handbook. Students and employees who utilize artificial intelligence are accountable for any AI output they generate and share. AI must not be used to generate, facilitate, or assist with any illegal activity.

## Data Sensitivity

The School places the highest priority on student and employee privacy. Faculty, staff, and students must avoid entering or uploading sensitive personal or confidential information into AI systems. This includes names, social security numbers, home addresses, other identifiable details, and any secure data.

## Student Use of AI

Students are expected to use generative AI openly and transparently. With the support and approval of their teacher, students may leverage AI tools to assist them in learning core skills and content. While generative AI can be utilized as a means to assist student learning, it should never be used to replace original student work or thought or subvert the goals of a given assignment or in-class activity. Students who represent text or any other form of content generated in part or whole by any AI tool as their own are in violation of the school's honor code and will be subject to a disciplinary response. If a student has any questions about their use of AI on an assignment, they should contact their teacher prior to enlisting the help of an AI tool.

## AI, Sourcing, and Research

Given its tendency to produce inaccurate information, text generated by artificial intelligence does not meet the threshold of scholarly work and should not be cited in academic essays. With the approval of their teacher, students may use AI to help curate sources. However, they should never rely on AI-generated text or images as reliable sources for their work.

## Student AI Honesty Pledge

> "We pledge that all work included in this assessment was produced by the student and does not contain any AI-generated material. Any use of artificial intelligence in the academic process was done in accordance with the School's AI Policy and the teacher's expectations."
